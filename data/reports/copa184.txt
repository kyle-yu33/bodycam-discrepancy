I responded to assist other units on the 1400 block of Lawndale. The subject was standing next to the driver's side of a black SUV with his hands cuffed behind his back. Several officers were standing around the subject. I asked another officer to tighten the subject's handcuffs. Officers discussed a magazine found in the area. The subject appeared agitated and uncooperative. The subject dropped to the ground and had to be lifted back to his feet. A sergeant arrived with tape to close off the block. When officers checked his handcuffs, the subject pulled away from them. I feared the subject might try to run. Before my camera was activated, the subject discarded a magazine near the vehicle.
